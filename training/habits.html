🎯 Band 7 Habits

The Habits of High-Scoring Writers

Instead of memorizing 1,000 difficult words, learn 100-200 useful words extremely well and understand their:

  • Synonyms
  • Collocations
  • Word forms
  • Grammatical usage
  • Context

Instead of forcing complex sentences:

Learn to produce:

  • Accurate simple sentences
  • Compound sentences
  • Complex sentences
  • Varied structures naturally

Accuracy first ➔ variety second ➔ sophistication where appropriate.
